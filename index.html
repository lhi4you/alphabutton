Help Center

Common Questions

How do I install AlphaButton?
Download the installer from your purchase email, close 3ds Max, run the installer, then restart 3ds Max. AlphaButton appears as a dockable panel you can launch from the plugin menu.
It says "Select objects first" — what now?
AlphaButton generates drawings from your current selection. Select one or more objects in the viewport and the Generate button activates, showing the object count.
Why am I getting zero-thickness warnings?
Some geometry contains faces with no depth, which can't be dimensioned reliably. Enable "Include Complex Volumetric Part/Elements" under Detail Drawings, or add thickness to the offending objects, and re-generate.
Where are my settings saved?
Use "Save settings" in the Plugin Settings rollout to persist your title block, sheet and output preferences between sessions. "Restore default settings" reverts everything to factory defaults.
